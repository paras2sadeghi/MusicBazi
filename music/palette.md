## Splice

**Use it for:** trip-hop breaks, drum one-shots (909/Linn kicks, gated snares),
quiet texture layers (atmospheres, noise, field recordings), vocal chops.

**Don't use it for:** hooks and arps (those stay Vital + Hirajoshi), or full
loops as a track's backbone.

**How it works:**

1. Agents suggest exact searches (e.g. "dusty break 85 bpm", "gated snare 80s",
   "dark atmosphere texture"). I search and download in Splice.
2. My Splice download folder is added to Live's browser **Places** as `Splice`.
3. Agents may browse and load samples from that Place through Live's browser —
   nowhere else on disk.
4. **Shape every sample** (pitch, filter, chop, envelope, FX) — same rule as presets.

Splice samples are royalty-free for my releases but not redistributable; audio
stays out of git (`.gitignore`).

## Sound character *(interview)*

Cold 90s, not warm analog:

- **FM / cold digital** — glassy bells, DX7-style keys, Wavestation-like digital pads.
- **M1-era romplers** — organ, piano, workstation strings and choirs.
- **Acid / mono bass** — 303-style squelch, analog mono lines.
- **Effects:** big dark reverbs + delays, 80s chorus / flanger, grit and saturation.
- **Never leave a raw preset.** Shape every sound (filter, envelope, FX, automation)
  — "sounds feel cheap" is a pain point.
