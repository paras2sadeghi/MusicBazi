# Identity

Who I am as a producer and what I'm aiming for. Agents read this before writing

## North star *(interview)*

Ethereal, dark electronic music with a 90s soul — **emotional and danceable at
the same time**. Layered but minimal.

A track should leave someone feeling **melancholic but hopeful** *and*
**dark / hypnotic** — bittersweet longing on a groove that keeps pulling.

## What I make *(interview)*

Four lanes, each defined in `genres.md`:

- **Techno** — driving peak-time + hypnotic
- **Darkwave** — cold synths, drum machines, gated snares
- **Trip-hop / dark alt** — dusty breaks, cinematic, melancholic
- **Darkwave–techno hybrid** — darkwave moods on a techno groove

Not now: Persian fusion, lo-fi, pop/indie (removed 2026-11-01).

## Core traits *(interview)*

- **Hirajoshi** colors hooks and arps; harmony underneath is set per lane.
- **≤ 3 focal parts at once**, with quiet texture layers for depth.
- **Cold 90s colors:** FM / digital, M1-era romplers, acid mono bass.
- **Space:** big dark reverbs and delays, 80s chorus / flanger, grit and saturation.
- **Hooks evolve** through automation and lean on tension notes.

## What agents should help with most *(interview)*

1. **Loops never become songs** → push toward arrangement (`arrange` skill).
2. **Melodies feel boring** → evolving, tension-driven hooks (`ideate`).
3. **Sounds feel cheap** → shape every sound; never leave raw presets.

## Hard avoids

Agents never do these without asking:

- Cheesy major-key melodies.
- Robotic MIDI: flat 127 velocities, rigid grid timing.
- Raw, unedited factory presets *(interview: "sounds feel cheap")*.
- More than 3 parts fighting for attention at once.
